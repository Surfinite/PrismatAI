# Our numbers — v2 (additive body-floor + production; expect iteration)

Generated by `docs/scratch/gen_our_numbers_v2.js` (run it to regenerate). This table is the **per-unit defensive value** `ours()`. These OURS values feed the defense-eval sim (`eval/defense/`), which adds a **prime-defender keep-value** — a perpetual future-absorb credit on the surviving anchor plus a room-capped credit for an untouched below-max healer — that is **not** shown in this table. See `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-06-25-prime-defender-keep-value-design.md`.

**Constants** — BV 2.2/HP (block value per soak) · **ATK = BV/R_HALF ≈ 1.91/pt** (derived: my block lands this defense phase, an attack a half-turn later) · THREAT 0.1/atk.

**Clock** — full-turn interest R = 4/3 ≈ 1.33 · half-turn R_HALF = √(4/3) ≈ 1.15 with discount d² = 0.75 · perpetuity P = 1/(1−1/R) = 4 (once-per-turn stream).

**Resources (WILL)** — gold 1 · green 1.33 (Conduit) · blue 1.67 (Blastforge) · red 1 (Animus) · energy 0.3 (hedged, board-dependent). Green/blue/red are producer-derived = gold-cost / (output × 3).

**Value = block_floor + production:**
- **block_floor** = effective-soak-HP · BV (PERMANENT; lifespan==1 → 0). For a **healer**, effective-soak-HP is the *discounted multi-turn climb to max* — current HP + each future turn's heal gain (cumulative-capped at max) discounted by d^(2t) — **not** just one heal.
- **net** = attack·ATK − abilityCost − **abilitySac PRODUCTION** (Odin→Treant, Plasmafier→Drone; the sacced unit's body is excluded — it isn't a prompt defender, ~10-turn soak ≈ 0).
- **production** — auto net·P · click net·(P−1)+THREAT·atk (taps → forgoes the one death-eve soak attack) · charge net·geom(ch) (preserved STOCK, no penalty) · doomed net·geom(life−1)+THREAT·atk · economy resource·{auto P / click P−1}.
- The **block** and **atk** columns sum to **OURS**. Charge units are shown at FULL charge (per-charge breakdown + convergence below).

**Mechanics (tunable)** — self-sac max(block,burst)+opt (atk-burst 0.2 / token-burst 0.1) · token-spawn = production stream of created tokens (own +ours(token), build-time-discounted; opponent −block) · chill +targetAmount·ATK·0.5 (flat, board-aware later) · drone-kill +denied-drone production (Deadeye charge) · undef −0.5/HP block · fragile −0.1 block · Xaetron's HP-funded click ignored (→ pure block).

## Convergence check (charge ch→∞ vs the equivalent perpetual click)
Charges are a preserved STOCK (no death-eve penalty), so a charge unit at ch→∞ converges to **body + net·P** — which sits ~1 net-attack ABOVE the equivalent **perpetual click** (which pays the death-eve −1). That gap is the stock-vs-flow distinction (a held-back charge is never lost; a perpetual click forgoes its soak-turn attack).
